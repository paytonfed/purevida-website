Switching away from 5-gallon jugs sounds like a project. It isn't. Most of the businesses we serve across Dallas–Fort Worth, Austin and San Antonio started the same way: with a free, no-obligation trial that took about a week. Here's exactly what happens.

## 1. A quick assessment

A PureVida consultant visits your break room or kitchen, looks at where people actually drink water, ice and coffee, and checks the spot for a nearby water line and an outlet. We ask how many people you have and how much you go through today, so we can recommend the right system instead of the biggest one.

## 2. Installation in as little as 45 minutes

One of our local technicians installs the system on your existing water line. No jugs to haul, no storage closet to clear, and in most offices no downtime. Every system filters through multiple stages (our flagship water systems use 6-stage filtration), so the water tastes clean from the first glass.

## 3. One week of real use

Your team uses it like it's already yours for a full week: cold and hot water, ice if you chose an ice system, or bean-to-cup coffee with the PV12c. That week is the point. You'll know quickly whether people like it, whether the space works, and whether you miss the jugs (most don't).

## 4. A simple decision

At the end of the week we sit down, look at what you were spending on bottled delivery, and show the monthly number side by side. Businesses switching from bottled delivery typically save 30% to 80%. Filters, maintenance and service calls are all included with local technicians, so there's nothing else to manage. If it isn't a fit, you owe nothing. That's what "no obligation" means.
